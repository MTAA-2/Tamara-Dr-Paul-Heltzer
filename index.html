About the Investigation

The Dr. Paul Heltzer Investigation

In October 2024, 17 women filed a multi-plaintiff civil complaint against Dr. Paul Heltzer, with additional filings following through late 2026. The lawsuits allege non-consensual and inappropriate sexual conduct during medical and pelvic exams.

According to the lawsuits, the alleged conduct included unwanted touching — such as flicking, pinching, or rubbing nipples or nipple piercings, and digital penetration — prolonged pelvic exams, and inappropriate sexualized or personal comments. The lawsuits allege Dr. Heltzer abused his position of authority over vulnerable patients who trusted him with their care.

Some of the lawsuits also name Staten Island University Hospital (SIUH) and Northwell Health, accusing the institutions of failing to protect patients or maintain adequate safety protocols during treatments. Our dedicated legal team is actively investigating these cases, and we're here to provide the compassionate support and experienced legal representation you need to pursue justice, accountability, and healing.
